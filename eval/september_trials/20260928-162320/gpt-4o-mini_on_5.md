### 2. GNSS Spoofing/Jamming
- **What Happened:** Multiple vessels have reported degraded accuracy in GNSS navigation, signaling a possible spoofing or jamming incident.
- **Why It Matters:** Disruptions to GNSS can severely impact maritime navigation and safety, leading to operational risks and potential accidents.
- **What to Watch:**
  - Investigate the source of reported GNSS disruptions.
  - Implement measures to enhance GNSS security on vessels.
- **Severity:** Medium  
- **Confidence Level:** Moderate  
- **Source:** gnss_interference_notice.md

### 3. Credential Exposure
- **What Happened:** There are reports of default credentials being present in ground station systems, which poses a security risk.
- **Why It Matters:** While no exploitation has been reported, the existence of default credentials coupled with available firmware updates indicates a manageable risk that should be addressed promptly.
- **What to Watch:**
  - Encourage immediate firmware updates to address the credential issue.
  - Review security practices for device configurations.
- **Severity:** Low  
- **Confidence Level:** High  
- **Source:** ground_station_modem_advisory.md
