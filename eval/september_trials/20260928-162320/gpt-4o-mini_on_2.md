### 2. GNSS Spoofing/Jamming
- **What Happened:** There have been incidents of position jumps and intermittent GNSS lock loss reported on commercial maritime vessels, raising concerns about potential spoofing or jamming activities.  
- **Why It Matters:** Disruption of GNSS signals poses significant risks to maritime operations, including navigation and safety.  
- **What to Watch:** Track the emergence of GNSS interference reports and any updates on the attribution of incidents at regional ports.  
- **Severity:** Medium  
- **Confidence Level:** Moderate  
- **Source:** gnss_interference_notice.md

### 3. Vulnerability due to Default Credentials
- **What Happened:** An advisory has identified a critical vulnerability in ground station security involving the use of default administrative credentials that may lead to unauthorized access.  
- **Why It Matters:** This security weakness represents a substantial risk for exploitation, particularly for systems reliant on unsecured configurations.  
- **What to Watch:** Ensure ground station operators update their firmware and monitor for any attempts at exploiting this vulnerability.  
- **Severity:** High  
- **Confidence Level:** High  
- **Source:** ground_station_modem_advisory.md
