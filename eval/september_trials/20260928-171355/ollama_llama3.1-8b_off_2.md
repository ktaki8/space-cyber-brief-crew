--------------------------------------------------------

**Why it Matters:**
The exposed credentials pose a significant threat to satellite ground station operations, allowing potential attackers to gain unauthorized access.

**What to Watch:**

* Update default credentials immediately (Severity: Critical, Confidence: High, Source: ground_station_modem_advisory.md)
* Conduct thorough risk assessment for affected ground stations
