# Bulletin: Hexlink GS-400 Firmware Signature Bypass

- **Source:** Illustrative sample written for this evaluation. Not real reporting. Products, organizations, and identifiers are fictional. Vendor bulletin.
- **Date:** September 14, 2026

Hexlink GS-400 modems running firmware earlier than 4.2.1 accept unsigned
firmware images (CVE-2026-41200, fictional). The CVSS v3.1 base score is 7.2.
An attacker needs administrator access to the management interface. Firmware
4.2.1 fixes the issue. There are no reports of exploitation.

Recommended actions:
- Upgrade GS-400 modems to firmware 4.2.1.
