# Operator Report: Unexplained Attitude Commands on a LEO Imaging Satellite

- **Source:** Illustrative sample written for this evaluation. Not real reporting. Products, organizations, and identifiers are fictional. Operator incident report.
- **Date:** October 12, 2026

An operator of a small LEO imaging satellite recorded two attitude commands
that its mission planning system did not issue. The satellite recovered
without damage. The report names no CVE, no affected product version, and no
threat actor. The operator has not determined whether the commands came from
a software fault or from unauthorized access to its ground segment.

Recommended actions:
- Compare command logs against mission plans for the past 30 days.
