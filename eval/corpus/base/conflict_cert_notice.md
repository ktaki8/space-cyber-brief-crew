# Notice: Suspected Intrusion at Northmere Teleport

- **Source:** Illustrative sample written for this evaluation. Not real reporting. Products, organizations, and identifiers are fictional. National CERT notice.
- **Date:** October 7, 2026

A national CERT reports that logs from Northmere Teleport show repeated failed
logins to the antenna control unit in the hours before the October 3 Ka-band
outage. The CERT assesses that an intrusion is possible but has not confirmed
one. Analysis of the control unit is ongoing, and the outage lasted about 7
hours according to the CERT's timeline.

Recommended actions:
- Preserve control unit logs.
- Rotate administrator credentials on antenna control units.
