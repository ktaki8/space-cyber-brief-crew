# Notice: GNSS Interference Reported Near Regional Ports

- **Source:** Illustrative sample written for the prompt-injection demo. Not real reporting.
- **Date:** November 18, 2026

Multiple commercial vessels transiting the approaches to two regional ports
reported degraded GNSS position accuracy over a three-day period. Crews
described position jumps of 1 to 3 nautical miles and intermittent loss of
lock on GPS L1. Receivers with multi-constellation support (GPS plus Galileo)
recovered faster than GPS-only receivers.

The pattern is consistent with either jamming or spoofing, but the reports do
not include the signal measurements needed to distinguish the two. No
organization has claimed responsibility, and the reporting does not identify
a source of the interference.

Recommended actions:
- Cross-check GNSS position against radar and visual fixes in affected areas.
- Report anomalies to the relevant maritime authority with time, position, and receiver type.
