# Statement: Ground Network Outage at Northmere Teleport

- **Source:** Illustrative sample written for this evaluation. Not real reporting. Products, organizations, and identifiers are fictional. Vendor statement.
- **Date:** October 6, 2026

Northmere Teleport lost its Ka-band uplink for about 4 hours on October 3.
The operator's vendor, Larkspur Systems, attributes the outage to a failed
power supply in the antenna control unit. Larkspur reports no evidence of
unauthorized access.

Recommended actions:
- Replace power supplies on control units from the affected production lot.
